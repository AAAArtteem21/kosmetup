# GlowBook

**GlowBook** is a web-based appointment booking and management system for cosmetologists.

The platform allows clients to book beauty treatments online, manage their appointments, and view their visit history, while cosmetologists can manage services, schedules, bookings, clients, and business statistics.

The project aims to simplify appointment management, reduce scheduling conflicts, and provide a convenient booking experience for both clients and cosmetologists.

## Features

### Authentication and administration
- Register users through `POST /api/auth/register/`; successful registration returns access and refresh JWTs.
- Obtain tokens through `POST /api/auth/token/` with `username` and `password`, and refresh them through `POST /api/auth/token/refresh/`.
- Access the authenticated user's profile through `GET /api/auth/me/` with `Authorization: Bearer <access-token>`.
- Manage users in the Django admin at `/admin/`; create an administrator with `python manage.py createsuperuser`.

### Client
- Browse available beauty treatments.
- View prices and treatment durations.
- Check available appointment slots.
- Book appointments online.
- Reschedule or cancel appointments.
- View upcoming appointments.
- Access appointment history.

### Cosmetologist
- Manage beauty treatments and pricing.
- Configure working hours and breaks.
- Manage schedule exceptions and days off.
- View and manage appointments.
- Track client visit history.
- Mark appointments as completed or no-show.
- View business statistics.

### Booking System
- Automatic availability calculation.
- Appointment conflict prevention.
- Booking status management.
- Rescheduling and cancellation rules.
- Protection against concurrent booking conflicts.

### Notifications
- Appointment confirmation notifications.
- Cancellation and rescheduling notifications.
- Automated appointment reminders.

### Analytics
- Appointment statistics.
- Revenue reports.
- Cancellation and no-show statistics.
- Popular treatments.
- Client activity statistics.
